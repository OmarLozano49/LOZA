About Omar Lozano Zavala

From the plant floor to general management

I know that person. Because I've been inside that problem running plants across multinational manufacturing in LATAM. P&Ls between $50M and $70MM. Teams of 1,000+. Multi-country operations. Unionized environments where one bad decision costs you a week of production. I didn't advise from a PowerPoint. I signed the P&L.

Over that career, I delivered $1.2MM+ in annual recurring savings, trained 50+ Lean Six Sigma leaders, and led turnarounds in some of the most complex operating environments in the region.

What I learned after all of it: most mid-size plants aren't losing money because of bad people or old equipment. They're losing money because nobody ever built the structure that makes the operation legible: who owns what number, when it gets reviewed, and what happens when it misses. Without that structure, the best team in the world runs on instinct and reacts to yesterday's problem.
